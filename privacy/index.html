Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

This policy explains what Tempo collects, why it is used, who helps process it, and how you can ask to access or delete it.

Who operates Tempo

Tempo is operated by Andy Wu, an individual developer. For privacy requests, contact tempoenquire@gmail.com.

Postal address: Andy Wu, 82 James Carter Road, Mildenhall, IP28 7DE, United Kingdom.

Data you provide

Account data: If you sign in with Apple, we receive the user identifier, name, and email address you choose to share. If Google sign-in is enabled and you choose it, we receive the Google account identifier, name, and email address Google provides.

App content: We process the habits, categories, icons, schedules, reminders, completion counts, session timestamps, timer settings, notes, theme settings, and premium settings you create or change in Tempo.

Support data: If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include in the message.

Data collected automatically

Device and diagnostic data: We use Sentry for crash and error reports. Reports may include device model, operating system version, app version, stack traces, and technical context needed to fix bugs. Tempo configures Sentry not to attach default personal information.

Usage analytics: We use PostHog to understand app launches, screen views, feature usage, paywall events, timer events, and similar product interactions. We avoid sending habit names, notes, or other free-text habit content as analytics event properties.

Local storage: Tempo stores app settings, habit data, sessions, reminders, and authentication tokens on your device so the app can work offline and restore your session.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your card number or full payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, restore purchases, and unlock premium features. RevenueCat may receive purchase history, product identifiers, entitlement status, and the app user identifier used to connect your subscription to your Tempo account.

Sync and storage

You can use Tempo without signing in; in that case your habit data stays on your device unless you later sign in and choose to sync it.

If you sign in, Tempo syncs your profile, habits, completions, session timestamps, and account-backed settings to Supabase so you can restore and use your data across devices.

How we use data

We use data to provide the app, authenticate accounts, sync habits, schedule reminders, show progress and statistics, manage subscriptions, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, diagnose crashes, improve reliability, and understand which parts of the product are used.

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are performance of a contract for core app features and subscriptions, consent where required for optional permissions or analytics, legitimate interests for security, support, and basic diagnostics, and legal obligations where we must keep records.

Service providers

We use providers that help operate Tempo: Apple for App Store distribution, Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, and notifications; Supabase for authentication, database, and sync; RevenueCat for subscription management; PostHog for product analytics; Sentry for crash and error reporting; and Google only where Google sign-in is enabled and used.

These providers may process data in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area, United States, or other countries where they or their subprocessors operate. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, or equivalent protections.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your habit content for third-party advertising. We do not knowingly share personal data with data brokers. Tempo does not request location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data for its core habit tracking features.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Tempo schedules notifications on your device. You can turn notifications off in Tempo or in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, sign out, reset app data, or uninstall the app. Signing out removes local account data from that device, but it does not cancel subscriptions or automatically delete synced server records.

Synced account data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and associated server data, open Settings > Account > Delete account, or email tempoenquire@gmail.com. We may keep limited records where required for security, legal, tax, accounting, dispute, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

EEA, UK, and Swiss users may also have the right to complain to a local data protection authority. California and other US state residents may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and opt out of certain uses of personal information where those laws apply.

To exercise privacy rights, email tempoenquire@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to verify your request.

Children

Tempo is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used by anyone below the minimum digital-consent age in their country without any required parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can delete it.

Automated decisions

Tempo does not use personal data to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Changes

We may update this policy as Tempo changes. The latest version will be posted here with a new last-updated date.
